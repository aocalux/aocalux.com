What is JuliaPri.pink?

JuliaPri.pink is my personal website and bridge between the real world and my digital space. It is a place where I share my projects (stuff that isn't related to coding mostly), ideas, and a little bit of my personality online. The site acts as a simple portfolio and landing page for my online presence, creative experiments, and links to the rest of my online corners.

You can visit it directly at www.JuliaPri.pink.

How I made it

I built this project by hand using plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Instead of starting from a template or framework, I wrote the code myself so I could shape the layout, styling, and behavior around the content I wanted to share. Keeping the implementation straightforward also makes it easier to understand, maintain, and update as the site ages.

I designed the layout to work across different screen sizes (mostly at least ;)), with particular attention to mobile devices. Flexible content areas, readable spacing, and clear navigation help keep the pages comfortable to browse on phones and tablets as well as on larger screens. I continue to refine the experience so the site stays simple to use while giving my projects and ideas room to stand out.
